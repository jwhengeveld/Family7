Watch Family7+ on your phone, tablet and Android TV.

Sign in with your own Family7+ account to watch live TV and catch up on every programme, wherever you are.

• Live TV with the TV guide: see what is on Family7 now and in the coming days.
• Every programme: new arrivals, Originals, children's programmes, Bible study, drama and more. Browse by category or from A to Z.
• Search with suggestions as you type.
• My list: the same list as on family7.nl.
• Chromecast: send a programme or live TV to your TV with one tap and control it from your phone.
• Android TV and Google TV: a dedicated TV app, made for the remote control.
• Picture-in-picture on your phone.

Whatever Family7 publishes on its website appears in the app straight away: new programmes and episodes show up automatically.

You need a Family7+ account. Don't have one yet? Create one at family7.nl; the first 10 days are free.

Privacy: the app collects nothing for itself. No ads, no analytics and no server in between; the app talks directly to family7.nl.

This is an independently made app for Family7+ subscribers. Questions about your account or the programmes go to Family7.

The programmes of Family7 are in Dutch.
